Assistive mobility kit

Detect the world
before you touch it, not after.

A low-cost, three-layer obstacle-detection kit for visually impaired users — a sensing cane, sensing glasses, and a companion app — built to cover ground, chest and head level in one pass.

The problem

A cane alone can't see above the waist.

Traditional white canes are excellent at reading the ground — kerbs, steps, potholes — but say nothing about what's coming at chest or head height: parked vehicles, open car doors, low branches, signboards.

0–90cm

Typical ground-level detection range of a standard white cane — nothing above it.

3

Independent hazard zones a pedestrian actually needs covered: ground, torso, head.

₹4,000

Target build cost for a kit that covers all three zones — a fraction of commercial alternatives.

How it works

Three devices, one continuous field of awareness.

Each component watches a different band of space and reports back through its own alert channel, so the user never has to interpret more than one signal at a time.

Ground → chest

The cane

A standard cane shaft handles ground-level obstacles by feel. A forward-facing ultrasonic sensor mounted below the handle extends that awareness up to chest height.

  • ControllerArduino UNO
  • SensorHC-SR04 (fixed, forward)
  • FeedbackBuzzer + vibration motor
  • Power9V block + 18650 cell
  • EnclosureTiffin-style box on shaft
Head level

The glasses

A frame-mounted sensor watches the zone the cane can't reach — head-height hazards like branches, signage and open doors — and alerts through a buzzer near the ear.

  • ControllerArduino (pocket unit)
  • SensorHC-SR04, frame-mounted
  • FeedbackBuzzer near ear
  • Power packSeparate pocket unit
In progress

The app

A companion app paired over Bluetooth, added to meet the project's judging requirements — the remaining piece of the build now that the hardware side is complete.

  • LinkHC-05 Bluetooth module
  • StatusIn development
  • RoleAlerts & settings companion
